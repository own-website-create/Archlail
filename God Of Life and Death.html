God of Life and Death

"All things begin, and all things end beneath the same gaze."

Among all divine beings, a supreme authority is required to oversee all of the gods to preserve the balance and order of existence. This responsibility belongs to the God of Life and Death, the Absolute Deity and the highest power recognized by all gods. Despite appearing as two distinct forms, they are one and the same being. The masculine form embodies the God of Life and The feminine form embodies the God of Death. Unlike other gods, no chronicles speak of its origin. There are no records of parents, no tales of childhood, and no legends of its rise to power. Such matters are forbidden to know. As the Absolute Deity, it stand beyond history and beyond the understanding of mortals and gods alike. No life story has been written. No beginning has been recorded. And perhaps, no being is permitted to ask.
